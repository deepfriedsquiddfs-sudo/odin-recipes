Chicken Karhai Recipe

Description

Chicken Karhai is a popular Pakistani dish made with tender chicken cooked in a rich, spicy tomato-based sauce. It is traditionally prepared in a deep, wok-like pan called a karahi.

Flavoured with ginger, garlic, green chillies, and aromatic spices, this dish is best served hot with naan or roti. It is a delicious meal for anyone who enjoys bold, savoury flavours.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 2–3 tbsp neutral oil, or substitute more ghee
  • 1 small onion (approximately 150 g), chopped
  • 8 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3/4 inch ginger
  • 1–2 green chili peppers
  • 2–2.2 lbs (approximately 907 g) skinless, bone-in chicken
  • 8 small tomatoes (approximately 600 g), diced
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder, or to taste
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2–3 green chili peppers, for garnish
  • 1–2 tbsp fresh ginger, for garnish
  • 1 tbsp fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Heat ghee and oil in a large pan over high heat. Sauté onions for 5–6 minutes until lightly golden. Add garlic, ginger, and a whole green chili; cook for 1 minute.
  2. Add chicken and ½ tsp salt. Sauté for 5 minutes until the chicken changes colour and stops releasing water. Add tomatoes, coriander, cumin, chili powder, and remaining salt. Cook for 1 minute.
  3. Reduce heat to medium, cover, and cook for 20 minutes, stirring halfway through.
  4. Uncover and increase heat to high. Sauté for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked and excess liquid has reduced.
  5. Reduce heat to medium. Stir in freshly ground black pepper and garam masala. Garnish with green chilies, julienned ginger, and cilantro. Serve hot with roti, naan, bread, or rice.
