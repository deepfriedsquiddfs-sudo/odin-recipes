Onion Rings Recipe

Description

Onion Rings are a popular crispy snack made with thick slices of onion coated in a seasoned batter and breadcrumbs. They are deep-fried until golden brown and crunchy on the outside while remaining soft and tender on the inside.

Flavoured with paprika, garlic powder, and black pepper, these delicious onion rings make a perfect snack or side dish. They are best served hot with ketchup, mayonnaise, or your favourite dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 large onions
  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup cold milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/2 cups breadcrumbs
  • Vegetable oil, for deep frying
  • Ketchup or mayonnaise, for serving

Steps

  1. Peel the onions and cut them into thick slices approximately 1 cm wide. Separate the slices into individual rings.
  2. In a large bowl, combine plain flour, cornflour, baking powder, paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, and salt. Mix well.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the cold milk and egg. Slowly pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and whisk until a smooth batter forms.
  4. Place the breadcrumbs in a separate shallow bowl. Dip each onion ring into the batter, allowing excess batter to drip off, then coat it evenly with breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat vegetable oil in a deep pan to approximately 180°C. Carefully fry the onion rings in small batches for 2–3 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove the onion rings using a slotted spoon and place them on a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil.
  7. Serve immediately while hot and crispy with ketchup, mayonnaise, or your favourite dipping sauce.
